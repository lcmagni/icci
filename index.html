01 — The Research

When language becomes a measurable strategic asset

For decades, management scholars have argued that organizational language matters — that the words leaders use to articulate purpose shape culture, attract talent, and build trust. But "mattering" is not the same as being measurable.

The ICCI study (Magni, 2025) changes this. Drawing on a corpus of vision and mission statements from 65 US corporations across 10 GICS industry sectors, the research establishes a statistically significant, empirically verified relationship between a company's strategic linguistic clarity and its excess stock returns — the portion of performance attributable to the firm itself, isolated from sector-wide movements.

The Integrated Composite Clarity Index (ICCI) — explicitly designed as a proxy for symbolic intelligence, a latent organisational capability — combines two distinct dimensions: the neurosemantic coherence between vision and mission (CSDI, from Learnable Theory's 81-configuration matrix) and the linguistic readability of both statements (Flesch Reading Ease and Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level, equally weighted). Together they predict performance with R² = 0.183 (p < 0.001) in the linear model, rising to R² = 0.230 in the quadratic specification that reveals the full inverted-U relationship. The CSDI baseline alone achieves r = +0.370, R² = 0.137, p = 0.002; the ICCI composite improves this to r = +0.428, R² = 0.183, p < 0.001 — a ΔR² of +4.6 percentage points.

Neurosemantic Coherence
The CSDI measures how well a company's vision and mission activate the same human need dimensions — Survival, Safety, Sociality, Self-Actualization — and the same affective polarizations. A high CSDI means the two statements form a unified symbolic attractor.
Linguistic Readability
Readability metrics quantify how accessible the text is to a general educated audience. More readable statements lower information asymmetry, broaden stakeholder reach, and reduce the cognitive load required to form expectations about the firm.
The ICCI Composite
The ICCI integrates both dimensions into a single normalised index, capturing the interaction between semantic depth and communicative accessibility that defines genuinely effective strategic language.

02 — The Central Finding

Not more clarity — optimal clarity

The most consequential finding is not the positive linear association. It is the discovery that the relationship is curved. A quadratic regression reveals a statistically significant inverted-U: β(ICCI²) = −0.091, p = 0.020. The implication is profound.

Below the optimal zone, insufficient clarity leaves symbolic capital untapped. But above the optimal zone, excessive simplification depletes the culturally charged valences that give language its neurosemantic traction: messages become generic, undifferentiated, and perceived as lacking depth.

The goal is not to maximize clarity. It is to find the optimal clarity frontier — where semantic richness and linguistic accessibility are in productive equilibrium.

03 — This Application

From theory to actionable alternatives

This application operationalises the ICCI research. You provide your vision and mission statements, select your GICS industry sector, and the engine analyses and generates optimised alternatives — in pairs, comparing your current language with versions calibrated toward the optimal zone.

1
Score your statements — compute a detailed ICCI score with CSDI and readability breakdowns, showing exactly where on the inverted-U curve your language sits.
2
Profile the neurosemantic architecture — map which need dimensions (Survival, Safety, Sociality, Self-Actualization) are activated and how coherently vision and mission align.
3
Receive optimised alternatives in pairs — three pairs (light refinement, moderate reframe, deep optimisation), each showing your current statement beside a version calibrated toward the optimal zone for your sector.
4
Download the PDF report or print it — a fully formatted document for sharing with leadership teams, boards, or communication consultants.
